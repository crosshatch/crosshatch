Fold the `@crosshatch/util` and `@crosshatch/widget` packages into
`crosshatch`. Their contents are now available from the `crosshatch/util` and
`crosshatch/widget` subpath exports, and the standalone packages are no longer
published. Docs-only assets (fonts and the vocs theme) moved into the docs site
and are no longer shipped with the library.
